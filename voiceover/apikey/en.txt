To use AI features in AI Shiksha Mitra, you need your own free API key. It's a one-time setup, and we'll do it together.

An API key is simply your personal pass to Google's AI. It powers chat, tools and paper generation.

And it's free. No card, no payment.

In the app, tap More, then Your API key, and open the Google AI Studio link.

Sign in with your Google account, and tap Create API key.

Choose a project. If you don't have one, simply create a new project, give it any name, and select it.

Tap Create key, and copy your key.

Back in the app, tick the confirmation, paste the key, and tap Save.

Your key stays only on your device, never synced to any cloud account.

The free key is enough for everyday use. Need more later? You can choose Google's paid plan, anytime.

That's it. Your AI features are ready. Happy teaching!
