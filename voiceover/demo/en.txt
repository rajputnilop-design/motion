Namaste, teachers! This is your complete guide to AI Shiksha Mitra, the AI built for Bharat.

It works on your phone and on your laptop. Let's walk through it, step by step.

At the bottom, you'll find five tabs: Chat, Studio, Tools, Courses, and More.

Chat is where you meet Aasha, your teaching assistant. Type a question, attach a photo or a PDF, or use the mic.

Tap the voice button to simply talk to her. She replies out loud, with live captions.

Ask for a lesson plan, and Aasha guides you to the right tool.

In Tools, open Lesson Planner.

Pull a chapter from the CBSE or NCERT syllabus. Choose the class, the subject, and the chapter.

Pick the language for your plan, add details under More options, and tap Generate.

In seconds, you get a period-by-period plan: a board plan, questions to ask with expected answers, homework, and even local examples.

Copy it, or send it to Chat to make changes.

Now, question papers. Go to Studio, and tap Create new paper.

Step one: choose the board, class and subject. Step two: select the chapters.

Step three is the blueprint. Set how many questions of each type, and the marks for each. The total adds up for you.

Choose the difficulty and language, then tap Generate.

Your board-aligned paper is ready, with an answer key. Download it as PDF or Word.

Or tap Publish. Share the join code or link with your students, and they can take the test online.

Their answers are checked for you. Open any submission to see an AI evaluation report, and share it on WhatsApp.

The Tools tab has more helpers, like Content Assistant, Student Report Generator, and Smart Communication.

In The Lab, turn photos into files. Snap your marks register, and get a clean spreadsheet.

It also makes reports, attendance sheets and Word files from your notes, ready to download or open in Google.

English Speaking helps you practise spoken English. Aasha explains in your language, says it in English, and then you say it.

Preparing for Maha TET? Take a full mock test, or practise subject-wise, with official answer keys.

In Student Hub, add your classes and students.

And in Courses, learn AI step by step, with live training and recordings.

Under More, you'll find Analytics, Assessment, Banks, Books, the App Guide, and WhatsApp support whenever you need help.

That's AI Shiksha Mitra. Less paperwork, more time for teaching. Try it today, at A.I. Shiksha Mitra dot com.
